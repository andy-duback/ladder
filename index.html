Give a purchase date, the price you paid, or both. With only a date, the dashboard uses that day's closing price.

The rules this page applies
  • Change is measured from the price you paid, or from the closing price on the purchase date if you left the price blank.
  • Between two rungs, the nearer one already passed applies: up 45% follows the up-40% rule.
  • Each percentage is a share of what you hold in that position at the time.
  • A rung counts once. Mark it done and it stays quiet, even if the price leaves and comes back. Marking a rung done also covers the smaller rungs before it.
  • Add, Mark done, Undo and Remove each open a pre-filled GitHub issue. Submit it, then reload this page in about a minute.
